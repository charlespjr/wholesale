How it works

From listing to assignment, on autopilot.

Three steps. Zero cold calls. Your AI agent handles the grind while you approve the deals.

01

Feed it deals

Dealfeed scans on-market listings around the clock and surfaces only the properties that fit your buy box — price, spread, condition, days on market.

02

Remi works them

Your AI agent writes the offer, contacts the listing agent, negotiates within the limits you set, and follows up until you say stop. Every conversation saved.

03

You close

When a seller says yes, you get the contract, the comps, and the full conversation history. Sign, assign, collect your fee.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need any tech skills to use this?+

No. You set your buy box and approval limits in plain English during onboarding. Remi handles everything else, and you approve deals from your phone.

Does Remi actually talk to real agents?+

Yes — via email and SMS, in your name or your company's. Every message is logged, and you can set Remi to require your approval before sending counters above any threshold.

What about off-market deals?+

The base Remi is built for on-market (MLS-listed) deals today. Off-market sourcing and seller-direct outreach are on the roadmap, and Pro users can build custom Remis for other deal types now.

Am I locked into a contract?+

No. Everything is month-to-month and you can cancel anytime from your dashboard — no calls, no retention scripts.

What markets does it cover?+

Dealfeed currently covers on-market listings in 38 states, and we're adding more every quarter. You can run multiple markets from one account on Pro.
